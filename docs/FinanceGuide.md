# VertexERP AI - Finance & Accounting Platform User Guide

## Overview

The **Finance & Accounting Intelligence Platform** in VertexERP AI provides enterprise-grade financial management, double-entry general ledger accounting, accounts receivable (AR), accounts payable (AP), cash & bank management, expense claims, budgeting, tax management, fixed asset depreciation, and GAAP/IFRS compliant financial reporting.

---

## Core Financial Capabilities

### 1. Chart of Accounts (COA)
- Hierarchical account structure across standard accounting classes:
  - **Assets**: Cash, Operating Bank Accounts, Accounts Receivable, Fixed Assets
  - **Liabilities**: Accounts Payable, Sales Tax Payable, Accruals
  - **Equity**: Owner's Equity, Retained Earnings
  - **Income**: Operating Revenue, Sales, Services Revenue
  - **Expenses**: Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Operating Expenses, Salaries
- Supports multi-currency accounts, sub-accounts, opening balance tracking, and activation status.

### 2. General Ledger & Journal Entries
- Strict double-entry accounting enforcement: `Sum(Debits) == Sum(Credits)`.
- Automated posting from operational transactions (Invoices, Supplier Bills, Customer Payments, Expense Reimbursements).
- Manual Journal Vouchers for accruals and adjustments.
- Instant single-click Reversal posting.
- Ledger running balances and fiscal period management.

### 3. Accounts Receivable (AR) & Accounts Payable (AP)
- **Customer Invoices**: Create sales invoices with line item details, taxes, due dates, and automated GL revenue posting.
- **Supplier Bills**: Record vendor bills with automated expense/AP GL posting.
- **Payments & Receipts**: Full payment lifecycle tracking for customer receipts and vendor disbursements.
- **Aging Analysis**: Dynamic 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, and 90+ day aging telemetry for receivables and payables.

### 4. Banking & Cash Management
- Track corporate bank accounts and current GL balances.
- Log bank deposits, withdrawals, and inter-account transfers.
- Interactive bank statement reconciliation wizard.

### 5. Expense Management
- Custom expense categories linked to General Ledger accounts.
- Employee expense claim submission and receipt attachments.
- Two-stage approval and reimbursement workflow.

### 6. Budget Management
- Fiscal year annual and departmental budget setup.
- Real-time Budget vs Actual progress tracking and variance analysis.

### 7. Fixed Assets
- Fixed asset register tracking purchase cost, salvage value, useful life, and book value.
- Support for Straight-Line and Declining-Balance depreciation methods.
- Asset disposal processing.

---

## Statutory Financial Reports

- **Trial Balance**: Complete debit/credit listing verifying mathematical balance.
- **Balance Sheet**: Statement of Financial Position enforcing `Assets = Liabilities + Equity`.
- **Profit & Loss Statement (Income Statement)**: Revenue vs Expense Net Profit computation.
- **Statement of Cash Flows**: Operating, Investing, and Financing cash movements.
- **Aging Reports**: Receivable and Payable aging buckets.
